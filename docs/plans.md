# Nimbus Notes Plans

## Free plan
The Free plan includes up to 3 notebooks and 500 MB of storage. Sharing is read-only.

## Pro plan
The Pro plan costs 299 INR per month or 2,990 INR per year. It includes unlimited notebooks,
50 GB of storage, offline mode, and edit sharing with up to 10 collaborators.

## Team plan
The Team plan costs 499 INR per user per month, with a minimum of 3 users. It adds an admin
console, single sign-on (SSO), and audit logs kept for 1 year.
