# Security and Data

## Data storage
All notes are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and stored in data centers in Mumbai, India.
Backups are taken every 6 hours and retained for 30 days.

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication (2FA) can be enabled under Settings > Security. If you lose access
to your 2FA device, use one of your 10 backup codes. If those are lost too, support can reset
2FA after verifying your identity, which takes up to 48 hours.

## Error codes
Error ERR-4021 means your session expired; sign in again. Error ERR-5003 means sync failed
because storage is full; delete files or upgrade your plan.
