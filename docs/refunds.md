# Refund Policy

## Monthly subscriptions
Monthly Pro subscriptions can be refunded within 7 days of the charge. After 7 days, the
subscription stays active until the end of the billing period and is not refunded.

## Annual subscriptions
Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days,
refunds are prorated for the unused full months, minus a 10 percent processing fee.

## How to request a refund
Email billing@nimbusnotes.example with your account email and invoice number. Refunds reach
the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
